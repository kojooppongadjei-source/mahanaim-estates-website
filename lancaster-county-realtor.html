The county, briefly

What makes Lancaster County different.

A county of small boroughs

Places like East Petersburg, Manheim, Marietta, Columbia, and Elizabethtown each have their own character, price range, and commute pattern. What counts as a "good deal" in one differs from the next.

Lancaster City itself

Rowhomes, converted rowhomes, and a genuinely walkable downtown, with a very different buyer pool than the surrounding townships. Historic character comes with historic maintenance considerations.

Farmland and rural properties

Amish and Mennonite farmland borders growing residential development throughout the county. Zoning, well and septic systems, and agricultural preservation restrictions all factor into these deals differently than a standard suburban sale.
