What's actually on the market

Three very different kinds of "Lancaster home."

City rowhomes

Walkable, often with original character, closer to Lancaster City's restaurants and downtown. Many need some updating; a few have already been fully renovated. Price and condition vary block by block.

Suburban and newer construction

Townships surrounding the city offer more space, newer systems, and often better school district options, at a different price point and a longer drive to downtown.

Rural and larger-lot properties

Further from the city, acreage becomes realistic. These sales often involve well and septic systems, and sometimes agricultural zoning considerations that don't come up closer in.

Finding the right one

How Eunice narrows a broad search.

Walkable city living, a quiet suburban street, or open space further out each come with real tradeoffs in commute, maintenance, and price per square foot. Getting this right early saves a lot of wasted touring.
