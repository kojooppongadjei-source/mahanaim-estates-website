What first-time buyers need to know

Pennsylvania has real help for first-time buyers.

PHFA assistance programs

The Pennsylvania Housing Finance Agency offers down payment and closing cost assistance for eligible first-time buyers, often through low-interest or forgivable loans. Eligibility depends on income and purchase price.

Low down payment options

Conventional loans can go as low as 3% down, FHA loans as low as 3.5%. The 20% figure most people assume they need often isn't the real requirement.

Closing costs, budgeted properly

Closing costs typically run 2 to 5% of the purchase price, on top of the down payment. Knowing this number early avoids a scramble right before closing.
