Financing

Six ways to fund the deal.

DSCR (asset-based) loans

Qualify on the property's rent rather than your personal income. Ratio = rent ÷ PITI; most lenders like 1.25, some go to 1.0.

HELOCs

A line of credit that uses the equity in a home you already own as collateral.

Conventional mortgages

Lowest rates; typically 15–25% down for an investment property; qualify on personal income and DTI.

Hard money

Short-term (6–24 month) loans for fix-and-flips and properties the bank won't touch.

Creative financing

1031 exchanges, and seller financing — including taking over a seller's existing low-rate mortgage payments.

The BRRRR method

Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat — how investors recycle the same capital into the next deal.
