Eunice Osei-Manu

Real Estate Agent, Mahanaim Estates at KW Elite

Eunice Osei-Manu leads Mahanaim Estates, a real estate practice built around a simple idea: buying or selling a home should feel guided, not confusing. Based in East Petersburg, Pennsylvania, she works with buyers and sellers on local sales, interstate relocations, and referrals across Pennsylvania and Maryland — through Keller Williams Elite.

The name Mahanaim comes from an old story about two camps meeting in the same place at the same time. It's a fitting name for the work: getting a buyer and a seller to the same page, the same number, and the same closing table.

Eunice's approach is plain-spoken on purpose. Contracts, financing, and negotiations are translated into language that actually makes sense, so every client understands exactly what's happening and why — before they sign anything.
