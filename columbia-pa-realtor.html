Columbia, Lancaster County

Real estate in a historic river borough.

Columbia sits on the east bank of the Susquehanna River, across from Wrightsville and about 28 miles southeast of Harrisburg. Once known as Wright's Ferry, it has a National Register historic district, a walkable downtown and trails along the water. We help buyers and sellers understand what makes Columbia different from the rest of the county.

Why buyers look at Columbia

A river town with a long memory.

History you can live in

Columbia was settled in 1726 and grew around the river, the first bridge across it to York, and the canal and railroads that followed. That history shows in the housing and the downtown, including Victorian and Art Deco buildings in the historic district.

Close to the river

The Susquehanna River Trail and Columbia River Park sit close to many downtown streets, which draws buyers who want to walk or bike to the water. Homes near the river should have their flood zone status and insurance needs checked early.

Its own small school district

Columbia is served by the Columbia Borough School District, a compact district of about two square miles with its own elementary, middle and high school campuses. Buyers should always confirm school assignment for the specific address.

Buying or selling here

What we pay attention to in Columbia.

Historic district rules

Columbia is a Certified Local Government with a Historic Architecture Review Board. If a buyer plans exterior changes such as windows, porches or siding in the historic district, we help find out early what review might apply, so there are no surprises after closing.

Older-home inspections

Many homes here are well over a century old. Roofs, foundations, wiring and heating systems get a close look, and buyers should budget for the upkeep that historic character brings.

Pricing from Columbia's own sales

Prices here do not move like the countywide average. We price listings and advise on offers using recent sales from Columbia itself, street by street where we can.
