Where Maryland differs

A few things that catch PA-to-MD movers off guard.

Transfer and recordation taxes

Maryland's combined state and county transfer taxes work differently than Pennsylvania's, and who customarily pays what can vary by county. This affects the real cost of a deal for both sides.

Disclosure requirements

Maryland sellers complete different disclosure and disclaimer paperwork than Pennsylvania sellers. Getting this right protects both sides of the transaction.

Local closing customs

Which party typically handles title work, how attorney involvement is structured, and standard timelines can all differ from what a Pennsylvania buyer or seller expects.

Working across both states

What that means for you.

No copy-pasted assumptions

Whether you're buying, selling, or doing both across the state line, we treat Maryland's process as its own, not a variation on Pennsylvania's.

Coordinated timing for cross-state moves

Selling in one state while buying in the other adds real timing complexity. That gets planned for explicitly, not discovered midway through.

Straight answers on cost differences

Taxes, fees, and typical closing costs get explained clearly upfront, so there's no surprise gap between what a Maryland deal costs versus a Pennsylvania one.
