Marietta, Lancaster County

Real estate in one of the river's quieter towns.

Marietta sits right on the Susquehanna, a small historic borough with a walkable Main Street, 19th-century brick homes, and a slower pace than the busier parts of the county. Eunice works Marietta regularly and knows what draws buyers to it, and what to watch for in its older housing stock.

Why buyers look at Marietta

A small river town with a distinct character.

Historic housing stock

Many homes date to the 1800s, brick and frame construction with genuine period detail. That character comes with older systems, so a careful inspection matters more here than in newer construction elsewhere in the county.

River-town pace

Marietta is quieter and more walkable than larger boroughs nearby, appealing to buyers who want small-town living without being far from Lancaster or Columbia.

A tight, specific market

Marietta's inventory is small. Homes here don't behave like the countywide average, so pricing and timing need to be read from Marietta's own recent sales, not a broader Lancaster County trend.

Buying or selling here

What Eunice pays attention to in Marietta.

Older-home due diligence

Roofs, foundations, knob-and-tube wiring, and heating systems get flagged early, before they become a surprise at inspection. Buyers should budget for what historic charm often comes with.

Pricing against Marietta's own sales

With a small number of transactions each year, pulling the right comps from Marietta itself, not a countywide blend, is what keeps a listing priced accurately.

Matching buyers to the town's pace

Marietta suits buyers looking for a specific kind of quiet, walkable living. Eunice helps buyers figure out early whether that fits, before touring homes that won't.
