How it works

Five steps, start to keys.

Figure out what "home" actually means to you

We start with a real conversation — budget, must-haves, deal-breakers, and the neighborhoods and school districts worth a look across Lancaster County.

Get pre-approved before you fall for anything

I'll connect you with lenders who move fast, so when the right listing shows up, you're ready to act — not scrambling for paperwork.

Search, tour, and cross homes off the list

You'll get new matches as soon as they hit the market at unis.kw.com, and we'll tour the ones worth seeing in person.

Write an offer that gets taken seriously

I'll price it with real comps, structure the terms to protect you, and negotiate directly with the seller's agent on your behalf.

Inspection, appraisal, and closing day

I'll walk you through every form, deadline, and inspection item in plain language, right up to the moment you get your keys.

Good to know

Buyer questions, answered plainly.

As fast as you need to. Some buyers tour their first home within days of that first conversation.

What does it cost to work with you?

In most transactions, the seller covers the buyer's agent commission — we'll go over the specifics together up front.
